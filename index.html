About Our School

MOLOLUWA MODEL SCHOOL
Afin Akoko, Ondo State

Mololuwa Model School is a reputable educational institution located in Afin Akoko, Ondo State, Nigeria. The school was established in 2006 by Miss Alabi Opeyemi with the vision of providing quality education and creating a strong foundation for children in the community.

From its humble beginning, Mololuwa Model School started with its primary section, which gradually grew and gained recognition through its commitment to academic excellence, discipline, good character, and quality learning. As the school continued to develop, more parents began to trust the institution with the education and future of their children.

In 2012, the secondary section of the school was acquired by the great man, Mr. Obalulowa Albert OBA, who continued to build upon the foundation that had already been established. Under his leadership and vision, the school experienced further growth and development, becoming one of the respected schools in the town.

Over the years, Mololuwa Model School has continued to grow in both academic and physical development. The school now has its own permanent building and facilities, providing students with a more conducive environment for learning.

Today, Mololuwa Model School continues to stand as a school committed to quality education, discipline, character development, and the preparation of students for a successful future. The institution remains an important part of the community and continues to contribute to the educational development of young people in Afin Akoko and beyond.

🎯 Our Vision

To provide quality and affordable education that develops students academically, morally, socially, and intellectually.
